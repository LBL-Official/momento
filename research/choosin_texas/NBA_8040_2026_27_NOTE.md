## Portfolio, expectancy, 4% / month

Payoff 20:40. Estimated S=450/604. Naive Kelly

```
b = 20/40 = 1/2
f* = (b·S − (1−S)) / b ≈ 23%
```

of bankroll per trade. That is not usable. Half-Kelly is still
~12%. The live MLB desk’s 12.5% of a weekly snapshot is a different
sport and a Risk cap, not a Kelly proof for this book.

Honest 1-contract candle-path on this sample: 2840¢ = $28.40 over
nine calendar months ≈ $3.16 / month. On a $50 bankroll that is
about 6% **average**, including December and June losses. It is not
4% every month.

Fees are not in the ledger. A few cents of fee can erase +4.70¢.
Maker-only, no-fill, and “order submitted ≠ fill” are all outside
this desk.

To *target* 4% of $50 = $2 / month you would need about 43
unit-contracts per month **if** EV stayed +4.70¢. Regular-season
FIRST80 count is about 50–115 / month. That is a sizing fantasy,
not a measurement. NBA is not a live Momento book. Position limits
and Risk are MLB.

   no pre-40 discretionary exit. Best-supported. Still not live.

3. **Q3 research path.** Measure 80/50 vs 80/40 on Q3 only on a
   season that was not used to rank the stops. Do not retune live
   80/40.
4. **Forbidden until PIT exists.** Any score-based exit after 80.
   Log it as `OPERATION_REQUIRED`.
5. **Risk.** Do not Kelly. Do not stack same-night size to hit
   4% / month. Preseason and Finals off. Treat December-style
   months as expected variance.

regular-season, no invented exits, and a **pre-registered**
watchlist (56–60¢ / ≥10 / 4–6) that is not promoted to a filter
until a season that was not used to fit it.

That is the opposite of guaranteeing 4% a month.
