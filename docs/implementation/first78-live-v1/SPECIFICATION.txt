9. Sizing epochs and overnight compounding (authoritative)
Sections 62–67 govern all sizing changes and supersede any fixed ten-trade batch interpretation elsewhere in this document.
A sizing epoch uses one frozen starting equity and one fee-inclusive 6% trade budget.
Ten distinct completed and fully reconciled trades in the current epoch set RESIZE_PENDING = true. They do not change sizing immediately.
Only during 01:00 <= America/Los_Angeles local time < 03:00 may a pending resize commit, after reconciliation passes and no entry construction is in flight.
Use the IANA timezone, including daylight-saving transitions, never fixed UTC-8.
Trades 11, 12, and later keep the old budget until an eligible resize commits. Epochs may contain more than ten trades.
At commit use an identified, authoritative reconciled equity snapshot. Atomically persist the new epoch, equity, 6% budget, timestamps, and reconciliation snapshot ID.
Do not carry excess completions forward as credit toward a second resize. Completion events count once, in the active epoch when reconciliation completes; retain the trade's original admission epoch separately.
Every admitted trade retains its original epoch and authorized acquisition budget, including partial entry fills after a resize. A resize never changes an existing position.
Missing or unresolved reconciliation defers resizing and preserves the prior budget.

11. Corrected exit policy
This replaces the previously unresolved exit ladder.
The desired exit mechanism is initially to acquire the opposing winner contract.
Assume:
we own Team A YES
Then:
hedge instrument = Team B YES
Before enabling this mechanism, the service must verify from market metadata that the two contracts are truly exhaustive and mutually exclusive under identical settlement rules.
Equal quantities of exact complements create a fixed settlement payout. Do not call that generally "delta neutral" in the ledger.
Call it:

because binary claims in incomplete markets should not automatically be treated like continuously hedgeable underlying assets. The prediction-market literature explicitly distinguishes market price from underlying physical probability and describes these contracts as incomplete-market binary claims. Yang_2026_Pricing_Prediction_Ma…

For exact opposing winner contracts in the same game, however, equal quantities can lock the terminal state payout if and only if the contract definitions are exact complements.

13. Correct hedge state machine
The old statement:
36 trigger -> 35 -> 34 -> ... -> 25
was incomplete because it ignored market direction.
Implement the following.
Branch A — recovery after trigger
If the hedge has triggered at opponent 36¢ and the opponent subsequently falls:
36 -> 35 -> 34 -> 33 ...
then the original position is recovering.
The passive opposing-contract bid may be ratcheted downward:
35
34
33
...
25
according to the current quote state.
Purpose:
lock the position at a progressively cheaper complementary acquisition price rather than unnecessarily paying 35¢ after the market has recovered.
Each reprice must:
1. cancel/replace safely;
2. preserve filled quantity;
3. avoid duplicate live exposure;
4. remain post-only;
5. preserve the target matched quantity.
This is the recovery hedge ladder.
It is not an adverse-price chase.

Branch B — deterioration after trigger
If the hedge triggers at opponent 36¢ but the opponent subsequently rises:
36 -> 37 -> 38...
our original position is deteriorating further.
Do not perform:
35 -> 34 -> 33
because those prices become less executable as the opponent rises.
Do not leave the system waiting indefinitely for a stale maker bid.
Instead:
cancel passive hedge orders
transition = EMERGENCY_DIRECT_EXIT
Close/reduce the original position using the authorized taker-capable fallback.
The purpose of the maker hedge is price improvement.
It must never convert a defined stop into unlimited adverse exposure because the passive order failed to execute.

30. Prediction-market pricing layer
Do not make the pricing model override FIRST78 V1 entry eligibility.

Instead create a parallel research/diagnostic module:

The Yang paper argues that prediction-market prices should not automatically be interpreted as physical probabilities: observed prices may contain a pricing wedge. Its proposed architecture combines a log-odds state variable, stochastic dynamics in log-odds space, and a Wang-transform decomposition. Yang_2026_Pricing_Prediction_Ma…
It also motivates log-odds because probability prices are bounded in \((0,1)\), whereas the logit maps them onto the real line. Yang_2026_Pricing_Prediction_Ma…

# 98. Exit-Equivalent Price

Because Momento may exit by buying the opposing contract rather than selling its original contract, normalize every exit into an **original-contract-equivalent price**.

### Direct exit

If original YES is sold at:

\[
x
\]

then:

\[
ExitEquivalent=x
\]

### Opposing-contract hedge

If Momento buys the exact complementary YES contract at:

\[
h
\]

then, before fees:

\[
ExitEquivalent=1-h
\]

Examples:

| Opponent hedge purchase | Original exit-equivalent |
|---:|---:|
| 35¢ | 65¢ |
| 36¢ | 64¢ |
| 40¢ | 60¢ |
| 45¢ | 55¢ |
| 50¢ | 50¢ |

Therefore your 50¢ minimum average-exit objective can be measured consistently regardless of which leg was used to eliminate exposure.

For mixed exits:

\[
ExitEquivalentVWAP
=
\frac{
\sum(q_i\times equivalentPrice_i)
}{
\sum q_i
}
\]

# 103. Why the 50¢ Average Exit Is a Strong Safety Metric

- 80% of positions ultimately return $1;
- 20% require adverse exit;
- average adverse exit = 50¢;
- entry = 80¢.

Before fees:

Winning contribution:

\[
0.80\times(1.00-0.80)=+16¢
\]

Adverse contribution:

\[
0.20\times(0.50-0.80)=-6¢
\]

Gross expectancy:

\[
\boxed{+10¢/contract}
\]

That is far above your +1.5¢ net-EV target.

So if **only 20% truly require adverse exits** and those exits genuinely average **50¢**, the strategy would have substantial economic room for fees and execution costs.

But that is exactly why the live ledger must measure these separately. If instead:

- more than 20% exit adversely;
- winners are sometimes exited before $1;
- entry VWAP drifts toward 82¢;
- hedge fills are poor;
- fees are higher;
- 50¢ exit-equivalent isn't maintained,

the economics change quickly.

# 109. Binding accounting and measurement definitions

These definitions qualify sections 92–108 and override their simplified planning examples.

- FIRST78 V1 remains a 78-cent midpoint signal with actual entry fills permitted only by its 78–82-cent execution policy. An 80-cent planning example is not a new entry trigger.
- Ten fully reconciled completions request resizing. Only a safe commit during 01:00 <= America/Los_Angeles time < 03:00 changes the active epoch. No scorecard, deadline or profit target may override that rule.
- NCAAB remains verified P5 vs P5 only. Measurement targets cannot broaden eligibility.
- January 1 reporting uses the deadline stated in section 92. Freeze a separate deadline snapshot even if fewer than 50 trades complete; freeze the 50th-trade report whenever trade 50 actually reconciles. Order completions by durable reconciliation sequence so simultaneous completions cannot duplicate or skip a checkpoint.
- Completed trade means all original exposure is exited or settled, outstanding orders are resolved, and all fills, fees and final P&L are reconciled. Fully paired but unsettled complementary contracts remain SETTLEMENT_NEUTRAL / SETTLEMENT_PENDING; their locked economic value is not realized trading profit.
- Report actual funded Kalshi equity, available cash, external reserves, cumulative net external flows, realized net trading P&L, and unrealized/locked-but-unsettled value separately. Starting strategy reference capital of $20,000 is not evidence that $20,000 was deposited to Kalshi. Moving a disclosed reserve into Kalshi is funding, not trading profit.
- Realized strategy return = cumulative realized net trading P&L / $20,000. The realized-capital objective is $20,000 + realized net trading P&L >= $21,000. Display the distinct account-equity objective alongside it. Do not claim all objectives passed unless the trade count, deadline, realized-profit and account-equity conditions each have authoritative evidence.
- Live realized EV in cents = 100 * total realized net P&L in dollars / total initial contracts for the same fully reconciled trade cohort. At the 50-trade checkpoint, exact net EV corresponding to $1,000 profit is 100000 / sum(initial_contracts) cents. If that denominator is zero, return UNAVAILABLE. +1.307 cents is only the specified 80-cent/five-batch approximation, not a universal target or evidence of the true expected return.
- Average adverse exit-equivalent is contract-quantity weighted across actual exposure-removing fills. Report the gross >=50-cent objective and the net value separately. Never mix favorable $1 settlements into this exit average. No qualifying exit fills means UNAVAILABLE, not zero or a pass.
- Direct-sale equivalent = sale price. Matched exact-complement equivalent = $1 minus opposing acquisition price. Count only matched quantity, never unmatched opponent holdings. Allocate actual fees to each quantity once. Include applicable realized settlement fees in final net economics. Do not count a later matched-pair settlement as a second exit fill.
- Split entry implementation shortfall versus the 78-cent strategy reference from execution shortfall versus the executable quote at submission. A midpoint or a passive unfilled order is not an executable fill benchmark. Retain timestamps and benchmark sources for both.
- The adverse-exit rate is trades with any qualifying exposure-removal fill / completed trades. Report the quantity-weighted adverse-exit share separately. Preserve partial exits and partial settlements rather than forcing every trade into an all-win/all-exit label. Track unhedged zero-payout settlements as losses in their own count and net P&L; never hide them by excluding them from the adverse-exit average.
- Below-50 reporting must include both exposure-removing contract quantity below 50 cents / total adverse-exit quantity, and adverse-exit trades whose exit-equivalent VWAP is below 50 cents / all adverse-exit trades. Label denominators. Keep median, minimum and tenth percentile definitions explicit and retain underlying rows.

The 50-cent mean objective is monitoring only; it cannot guarantee individual exit prices or justify delaying a protective exit.

Confidence intervals must identify the estimator, resampling unit and sample size; correlated trades from the same game/session must not be treated as independent contract observations. An observed 50-trade EV does not establish the long-run edge.
- Pace uses the actual authorized launch date and elapsed time. If launch date, schedule coverage or observed rate is insufficient, show UNAVAILABLE. The October 20 / 4.9-trades-per-week illustration is not the launch schedule. Scheduled games are candidate opportunities, not guaranteed eligible FIRST78 signals.
