# Edge decay

Historical \(\hat p=73.98\%\) is not a future constant.

Barrier-risk margin:

\[
m=\tfrac13-q.
\]

At \(q=26.02\%\), \(m\approx 7.32\) pp. At \(30\%\), \(3.33\) pp. At
\(33.33\%\), simplified EV is zero **before** realistic execution costs.

Stress \(q_t=q_0+\delta\) for \(\delta\) that close the margin. Do not
claim to forecast market-maker improvement. The health metric is the
margin, not a predicted \(\delta_t\).
