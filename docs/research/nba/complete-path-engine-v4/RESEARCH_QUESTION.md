# Research question

V1–V3 tested **states** (at 80 or after 80).

V4 tests **functions**:

```text
X = (K_s, G_s)_{s ≤ τ80}
Y = Y_40_CLOSE
```

Question:

```text
Is the distribution of arrival paths among the 320 failures
different from the distribution among the 910 survivors
in a way that is chronological, calibrated, and economically usable?
```

Not:

```text
Can we find one more scalar at τ80 that correlates with Y on TRAIN?
```

Momento still does **not** require predicting the NBA winner.

The engine is designed so that **no conditional signal** is an acceptable,
publishable answer. Do not iterate OOS. Do not promote overlapping mean
curves as a filter.
